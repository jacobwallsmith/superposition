Rules

This is a 2 player game. Win at least 2 out of 3 rows to win the overall game.

Take turns writing a number from 1 to 10 in any empty box, in any order, including those in your opponent's column. Each number may only be used once. Remaining numbers are displayed beneath the grid for ease.

Once the grid is full, each row's P1 and P2 numbers will be compared to find the winner of that row.

If the number in the 'Key' of that row and the thicker orange 'Master Key' have the same parity (i.e. both are odd, or both are even), the player with the lower number wins that row. If the Key & Master Key parity doesn't match (i.e. one is odd and one is even), the higher number wins that row.
